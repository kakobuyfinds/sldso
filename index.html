Buying guide

Shortlist and verify

A HooBuy spreadsheet is a starting point for finding product links. A useful shortlist records enough detail to identify the same item later, even when a seller edits the title or changes the default color.

01 · The listing

Compare HooBuy finds by item ID

Open HooBuy product links and record the marketplace, item ID, seller and selected color and size. If two rows show the same item ID on the same marketplace, compare the variants before treating them as separate finds. Reopen the listing before paying to catch stale links or changed options.

02 · The fit

Make a HooBuy size guide for your shortlist

Measure both feet in centimeters and check whether the shoe chart uses foot length or insole length. For a hoodie, lay one you own flat and measure pit to pit. Compare T-shirt body length and jacket sleeve length from the same measuring points used in the listing.

03 · The details

Check fit beyond the size label

For shorts, compare the inseam and waist. For headwear, check the adjustment range against your head circumference. Check both pieces of a matching set and confirm the waistband measurement on underwear. For jerseys, ask for a straight-on view of any name or number print.

04 · The evidence

Read HooBuy QC photos as a set

Compare the warehouse photo with the catalog image and your selected variant. For shoes, request both sides, both heels and the outsoles. When checking a pair for mismatches, ask for both shoes in one frame. If a measurement matters, ask for a ruler in the photo.

Shipping notebook

Your first parcel estimate

Keep the CNY listing price separate from local delivery, service fees, currency conversion and international freight. Compare quotes for the same destination, parcel dimensions and service. Check whether the carrier bills on actual or volumetric weight and whether the quote includes destination charges.

Keep one record per item. Save the item ID, variant, order reference, warehouse arrival and QC status. If an order arrives in separate parcels, record the tracking number for each parcel.

When you have the packed dimensions, compare the quote again before authorizing dispatch.

Quick answers

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is usually a community-maintained list of product links organized by category. Entries may include a size chart, a listed price or quality control photos. Check the live listing for the exact variant.

What if a HooBuy product link opens a different item?

Compare the marketplace, item ID and selected variant with your notes. If you cannot match all three to the intended item, remove the link from your shortlist.

Can HooBuy QC photos prove a branded item is genuine?

QC photos can show visible condition, construction and labels. For authenticity, look for verifiable seller provenance or an independent authentication service.

Does the spreadsheet price include international shipping?

Check the cost breakdown at checkout. The listed item price may exclude local delivery, service or payment fees, international freight and destination charges.
